Rides & Journeys redesign

Thirty designs for /mygarage/rides, each with its own theme, motion and way in. One is six structures; two is six you operate; three is six objects; four is six scroll pieces; five is six more, from a pointer-lit night map to a dense sortable table. All thirty read the real six rides, and all of them had to solve the same problems: no ride has a photo, One Ride 2026 has one stop and no route, its distance is the string “TBD”, and two rides have no date at all. Use Phone to check any of them at 390 × 844.
Standalone HTML in this folder, all reading the real ride files through rides-data.js. Open from the file system — nothing to build.
