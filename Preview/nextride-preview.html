Next Ride card

Six replacements for the NEXT RIDE panel in the Rides & Journeys hero. None of them draws the route — the page behind already shows the road, so these spend their space on time, cost, company and what the day includes. Each reads the two real upcoming rides, and every card has a switcher at the bottom: flip between them to check the two shapes — One Ride has a logo and no start time, Dhondenling has a 5:30 AM muster and no logo file at all.
Standalone HTML in this folder, all reading the real ride files through nextride-data.js. Countdowns are live — open one and watch the seconds.
