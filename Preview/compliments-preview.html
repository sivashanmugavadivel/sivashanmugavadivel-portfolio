Compliments — twelve posters

Takes on the compliments panel — the inclusions list an organised ride's poster carries, rendered today by ComplimentsPoster in GarageV7RideDetail.jsx. 7–12 draw the perk: "tea/coffee + snacks" gets a cup with steam rising off it, "lucky rider wins" gets a trophy catching the light — the illustration is picked from the text itself, so it works on entries with no emoji. 1–6 are the earlier typographic set. Every one is animated, so use Replay to watch an entry sequence again. Switch rides from the dropdown: the Plain fixture has no icons and no organiser, and Long runs eight entries with two that wrap — between them they find the designs that only work at five short lines.
Standalone HTML in this folder, all reading compliments-data.js; 7–12 also read compliments-scenes.js for the drawings. The Dhondenling fixture is the real r7 ride, verbatim.
